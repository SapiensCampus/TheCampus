# UX audit — 9 Nov 2026

Student app at `web/` (Vite, port 5173). Admin at `admin/` (port 5174, Google sign-in only). Signed in with the test account in `.env`. Reviewed at 360×800 first. Findings below were written before the UI edits.

## High

1. **Quiz choices sit below the fold.** On a phone, the question card, coach line, and 96px stacked reactions push “Maybe” and “I’d love it” off screen. With a trivia strip, only “Not for me” is visible. Students can miss the positive answer.
2. **Pause can wipe the quiz.** Pause (and Back on question 1) opens the intro, whose button always starts a new quiz and clears answers.
3. **Welcome hides the sign-in.** On a phone the Ikigai diagram is ordered first, then two paragraphs. “Sign in with Google” sits on the bottom edge; email sign-in is below the fold.
4. **After a role is chosen, the next step is far away.** Confirming Builders · Hackers leaves careers, skill tags, and copy above the roadmap button (~1860px down on a 800px screen).
5. **Two suggested roles don’t fit with the action.** Each role card is ~315px. The second role and “Pick a role to continue” start below the fold. The legend is a long uppercase sentence.
6. **A failed invite error sticks.** “Invalid invite code” stayed on the intro screen after sign-in. Profile and results also repeat that banner above their own error.
7. **Step details repeat themselves before “Mark as done”.** The time is said twice, the first step adds a long paragraph, the resource adds several disclaimers, and the notes warning is a full policy line. The action is at the bottom of that stack.
8. **Back and Sign out are small.** Measured about 33×52px, under a 44px touch target.

## Medium

9. **Roadmap chrome competes with the trail.** “Version 1”, “0 finished this trail”, and “0 students currently here · 0 students have completed this step” on every step. Zero counts don’t help a student choose what to do next.
10. **Quiz chrome is noisy.** Round bars plus “01 / 30”, a coach line, “Your interest mix”, and “Tap a feeling…” say the same thing. Pause and Back both leave on question 1.
11. **Invite errors sit in a banner above the card**, so the form jumps and the message is away from the field.
12. **Loading uses a looping bounce** (`bump`, 0.9s infinite). Trivia fades in over 350ms. Button motion is 120ms and does not respect reduced motion.
13. **Profile hint is long** (“the same one you’ll join / get validated with…”). Four fields are fine; the hint isn’t.
14. **Admin** (same brutalist tokens, different app): nav and small buttons go down to 32–36px. Not signed in — admin is Google-only for `sapienscampus.in@gmail.com`. No admin code change in this pass.

## Low

15. **Landing is clear.** One primary, “Get started”. The Explore card repeats that path. “Next up” cards look similar but aren’t buttons. Leave the brand line (“Evelve”).
16. **Email form defaults to Create account** after “Sign up or sign in with email”. The toggle is there; it’s one extra read.
17. **Empty, loading, and roadmap error states exist** (boot screen, question load error, trail unavailable, note Saved / Not saved + Retry). Boot copy is fine once the bounce stops.

## Fixed in this pass

1–8 and 9–13 above, except admin tap targets. Pause now resumes an in-progress quiz. Invite and sign-in errors sit on the form, in plain language, and typed email is kept. The logo returns home. Zero student counts are hidden. Loading no longer bounces.

## Not changed on purpose

Quiz scoring, role resolution, Firestore, auth providers, and roadmap data. No gamification added. Avatars and step task copy kept.
